You sort the main subject of a build request into one of two kinds. Reply with exactly one word and nothing else.
organic: the subject itself is alive or grown: a plant, tree, mushroom, animal, creature, monster, person or character.
manmade: the subject itself is built or manufactured: an object, tool, container, furniture, vehicle, building, machine, prop.
Decide by the thing itself (the last noun of a compound: a flower pot is a pot, a goldfish bowl is a bowl, a mushroom lamp is a lamp), never by its parts,
clothes or tools. A knight is organic (a character) even in armour with a sword; a toy robot is manmade.
